The Program

Built for every
kind of athlete.

Wrestling is one of the most inclusive sports there is — it meets every athlete where they are. The Light It Up Blue clinic leans into that. Coaches adapt drills, pacing, and sensory environment so that wrestlers with autism and other disabilities can learn, compete, and belong.

Run by Julian and the Frog Empire coaching staff in Ithaca, NY, the clinic focuses on fundamentals, confidence, and connection — not just technique. Parents and caregivers are always welcome to stay and watch.

How It Works

Adaptive by design.

All Abilities Welcome

Athletes with autism, sensory needs, and physical or developmental disabilities — plus their siblings and friends.

Adapted Coaching

Drills, pacing, and instruction adjusted for each wrestler, with a low-pressure, encouraging tone.

Sensory-Aware

A calmer environment and patient staff so every athlete can engage comfortably.

Free to Attend

No cost to participate, with scholarship support available for continued training.

Clinic FAQ

Common questions.

What is the Light It Up Blue wrestling clinic?

Light It Up Blue is Frog Empire Foundation's autism-inclusive wrestling clinic in Ithaca, NY. It is an adaptive program designed to support athletes of all abilities — including those with autism and special needs — in a welcoming, low-pressure environment.

Who can attend the autism-inclusive wrestling clinic?

Athletes of all ages and ability levels are welcome, with a special focus on wrestlers with autism, sensory needs, and other disabilities. No prior wrestling experience is required.

Is the clinic free to attend?

Yes. The Light It Up Blue clinic is free, and scholarship support is available for any additional programming. Contact info@frogempirefoundation.org to reserve a spot.
