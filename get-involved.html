Get Involved

Step on
the mat
with us.

You don't have to wrestle to make a difference. There are multiple ways to join the mission — pick the one that fits.

01

Volunteer

Help run events, coach clinics, handle logistics, or lend your professional skills (design, legal, accounting, marketing). Any time you can give makes a difference.

Sign Up
02

Nominate an Athlete

Know a young wrestler who needs support? Nominate them for scholarship consideration. Coaches, family members, and mentors can all submit nominations.

Submit Nomination
03

Partner With Us

Businesses, schools, clubs, and community organizations — we're always building partnerships. Let's figure out how we can work together.

Start a Conversation
04

Donate Gear

Have wrestling shoes your kid outgrew? Gear you no longer use? We'll redistribute it to athletes who need it. Quality matters more than quantity.

Donate Gear
05

Host a Fundraiser

Run a tournament, host a dinner, organize a drive. We'll support you with materials, guidance, and promotion. Every dollar raised fuels the mission.

Pitch an Idea
06

Spread the Word

Follow us. Share posts. Tell a friend, a coach, a business owner. Awareness is free and it's how the movement grows.
