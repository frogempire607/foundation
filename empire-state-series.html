How a club hosts a stop

You own the clinic. We bring the series, the name on the flyer and the scholarship money so nobody on your roster has to sit it out.

1

Send the inquiry

Tell us your club, your mat space and roughly when you'd want to run it. Two minutes, no commitment.

2

You set the terms

Age group, clinicians, session count and pricing are your call. We help book the clinician and build the flyer.

3

We cover the gap

Families apply to the scholarship fund for 50% or 100% off. The Foundation pays the club directly.

Questions we get

Who keeps the clinic revenue?

The host club. The Foundation's cut goes directly to supporting those who need it — we fund scholarships and help cover clinician costs.

Do you have to be a Frog Empire wrestler?

No. Every clinic in the series is open to wrestlers from any club in New York.

Why does the scholarship need a coach?

One short call with a coach confirms the wrestler is training and that the need is real. It keeps the fund going to the kids it's for.

How fast do you decide?

Within 2 business days of reaching your coach, and always before the clinic's registration closes.
