About The Foundation

We don't
make excuses.
We make wrestlers.

Frog Empire Foundation Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Ithaca, NY — built to remove the barriers keeping young athletes off the mat.

Our Story

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity is not.

Frog Empire Foundation was built out of a simple realization: talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not.

After launching Frog Empire Wrestling Academy, we saw firsthand how many athletes had the drive to succeed but lacked access — whether it was training, equipment, or the financial support to compete. The Foundation was created to bridge that gap.

Our goal is not just to build better wrestlers. It's to build disciplined, confident individuals who carry the lessons of the sport into every corner of their lives — work ethic, resilience, accountability, and the belief that hard work pays off.

Our Mission

What we stand for.

Frog Empire Foundation exists to provide access, opportunity, and support for young athletes through the sport of wrestling. We aim to remove financial and structural barriers so every athlete — regardless of background — can train, compete, and grow both on and off the mat.

01

Access

Elite training, coaching, and mats open to athletes who'd otherwise be locked out by cost or geography.

02

Opportunity

Scholarships, travel, and tournament support so effort — not economics — decides who competes.

03

Character

Discipline, respect, and confidence built through the sport, carried off the mat into life.
