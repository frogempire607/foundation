Wrestling
scholarships in Ithaca.

The cost of wrestling — tournament fees, club dues, camps, shoes, travel — forces too many talented kids off the mat. Frog Empire Foundation covers those gaps for athletes across Ithaca and the Finger Lakes, so effort decides who competes, not a family's budget.

Who It's For

Every level.
Every kid with drive.

Our wrestling scholarships support student-athletes from kindergarten through 12th grade who face financial or structural barriers to the sport. We serve families in Ithaca, Tompkins County, and the wider Finger Lakes region of New York.

We weigh commitment and character over win-loss records. If a young wrestler has the drive but not the means, we want to hear about them.

  • Youth wrestlers (K–6th grade) — fundamentals, first tournaments, and club fees.
  • Middle school wrestlers — camps, club dues, and competition travel.
  • High school wrestlers — private lessons, off-season training, and gear.
  • Underserved & adaptive athletes — including wrestlers with autism and special needs.
What It Covers

The costs that
push kids out.

A wrestling scholarship from Frog Empire Foundation can cover any of the expenses that most often end a young athlete's season.

Tournament & Club Fees

Entry fees and monthly club dues that add up fast across a season.

Gear & Wrestling Shoes

Proper shoes, singlets, headgear, and practice apparel.

Camps & Private Lessons

Summer camps and one-on-one coaching for athletes ready to level up.

Travel Costs

Gas, lodging, and registration for out-of-town tournaments.

How To Apply

Nominate a wrestler in
three steps.

  1. Tell us about the athlete using the nomination form or by emailing info@frogempirefoundation.org.
  2. We follow up to understand the athlete's goals and the barriers they're facing.
  3. If it's a fit, we put support in place — directly with the club, camp, or vendor.

Applying is always free. Parents, coaches, teachers, and community members are all welcome to nominate a young wrestler.

Scholarship FAQ

Common questions.

Who qualifies for a Frog Empire wrestling scholarship?

Youth (K–6th grade), middle school, and high school wrestlers in Ithaca, NY and the surrounding Finger Lakes region who face financial or structural barriers to training and competing. Effort and commitment matter more than win-loss record.

What costs does a wrestling scholarship cover?

Scholarships can cover tournament entry fees, club dues, private lessons, summer camps, wrestling shoes and gear, and travel costs — the expenses that most often force talented athletes to quit.

How do I apply for or nominate an athlete for a scholarship?

Use the nomination form on our Get Involved page or email info@frogempirefoundation.org. Parents, coaches, and community members can all nominate a wrestler, and athletes can apply on their own behalf.

Does the scholarship cost anything to apply for?

No. Applying for or nominating an athlete for a Frog Empire Foundation scholarship is completely free.
