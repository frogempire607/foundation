What We Do

Programs
built to level
the mat.

Four pillars that attack the barriers keeping young athletes from reaching their potential in wrestling.

01 · Scholarships

Financial aid
for young wrestlers.

Tournament fees, club dues, private lessons, summer camps — the cost of wrestling adds up fast. Our scholarship program covers those gaps so talented athletes aren't forced to quit because of cost.

Scholarships are available for youth (K–6th grade), middle school, and high school wrestlers. We're launching our expanded 2026 scholarship initiative with increased funding and broader eligibility.

Nominate an Athlete
02 · Academy Access

Train where
it matters.

Frog Empire Wrestling Academy is our flagship training facility — and through the Foundation, athletes who couldn't otherwise afford it get full access.

That means elite coaching, proper mats, and a culture of relentless development. We cover membership costs for scholarship athletes so the only thing they have to bring is effort.

Learn More
03 · Equipment & Travel

Gear up.
Show up.

Wrestling shoes, singlets, headgear, knee pads — the gear list is longer and pricier than most people realize. On top of that, the best competition is often a tournament or camp away.

We provide equipment support and travel stipends so that no athlete misses a match because they couldn't afford shoes or a bus ticket. Serious competition shouldn't have a price tag for a kid.

Fund Gear
04 · Clinics & Events

Build the
community.

We host clinics and community events throughout the year — technique sessions with elite coaches, open mats for wrestlers who don't have a team, and free events focused on development and inclusion.

These aren't just workouts. They're environments where kids meet mentors, build friendships across schools, and see that there's a whole community behind them.

Who We Help

Every level.
Every kid with drive.

K–6

Youth Wrestlers

The earliest levels — where habits, love for the sport, and fundamentals are built. Our entry-point programming focuses on fun, technique, and character.

MS

Middle School

The critical years where athletes either level up or fall away. We keep them in the sport with access to coaching and competition that schools can't always provide.

HS

High School

Elite development, recruiting support, and the final push toward college wrestling. We bridge the gap between varsity programs and the next level.

ALL

Underserved Athletes

The kids who have the fire but not the funds. Priority goes to wrestlers for whom cost would otherwise mean quitting.
