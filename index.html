The Mission

More Than a Sport.
A Foundation for Life.

Frog Empire Foundation exists to provide access, opportunity, and support for young athletes through the sport of wrestling. We aim to remove the financial and structural barriers so every athlete — regardless of background — can train, compete, and grow both on and off the mat.

Our goal isn't just to build better wrestlers. It's to build disciplined, confident individuals who carry the lessons of the sport into every corner of their lives.

What We Do

Four Pillars.
One Mission.

We attack the barriers that keep kids off the mat from every angle — so effort, not economics, decides who gets the chance to compete.

01

Scholarships

Financial support for youth and high school wrestlers who need a hand reaching their potential. Launching our 2026 scholarship initiative.

02

Academy Access

Training opportunities through Frog Empire Wrestling Academy — elite-level coaching made accessible regardless of background.

03

Equipment & Travel

Gear, shoes, and travel cost support. No wrestler should miss a tournament or practice because of what they can't afford.

04

Clinics & Events

Community clinics and development events focused on skill-building, mentorship, and inclusion for athletes at every level.

Our Origin

Why This Exists.

Frog Empire Foundation was built out of a simple realization: talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not.

After launching Frog Empire Wrestling Academy, we saw firsthand how many athletes had the drive to succeed but lacked access — whether it was training, equipment, or financial support. The Foundation was created to bridge that gap.
