Empire State Wrestling Series · For Families

Clinic scholarship
application.

If the cost of a clinic is what's standing in the way, ask. Scholarships cover 50% or 100% of registration, and we pay the host club directly — your wrestler walks in like everyone else.

Takes about 10 minutes Needs one coach reference Decision in 2 business days

Everything is required unless marked optional. Takes about ten minutes.

1Which clinic

An award covers the clinic, not a single session. The host club sorts out which session your wrestler is in.

2The wrestler

Clinics are open to ages 10 to 18.

3Parent or guardian
4What you're asking for
How much of the cost do you need covered?

A couple of sentences minimum. Read only by the two people who review applications — never published, never shared with your club.

5Coach reference — required

We call or email one coach to confirm the wrestler is training with you and that the need is real. One short conversation, nothing written.

6Before you send

You'll get a confirmation email right away.

What happens next

  1. 1We read your application — two reviewers, nobody else.
  2. 2We reach your coach for a short confirmation.
  3. 3You hear back within 2 business days, before registration closes.
  4. 4We pay the host club directly. Nothing marks your wrestler at the clinic.

No documents needed

We don't ask for tax returns or pay stubs. Your words and your coach's word are enough.

A different program

This fund covers clinic registration only. For gear, camps, and club dues, see our year-round wrestling scholarships.

Questions

Email info@frogempirefoundation.org or call (607) 327-7688. If a deadline has already passed, ask anyway.
